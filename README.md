Forecasts GTA VI's launch-day Twitch viewers hour by hour. Version 14 starts from v13 (A3 size-adaptive bandwidths + SIZE_REF 11.5 rolling-only early size gate on the verified v5 centre, convex early-dip echo, flat-diurnal level lift, size-adaptive spread) — every past launch curve re-indexed into unlock time (hours since the US East Coast midnight unlock, 04:00Z in daylight saving, 05:00Z in standard time; simultaneous-global launches use their own unlock hour, mixed console+PC-simultaneous launches are left out rather than aligned to the wrong hour), three centre members blended in log space (a kernel-weighted local-linear ridge of the log viewer-ratio on log hour-1 print, plus two kernel-weighted mean curves at different bandwidths), then corrected with the launch's own pre-release pattern: a one-sided diurnal dip, an inflation shift at the trough hours gated by the launch's own midday dip, an early-dip echo at midday, and a small-launch evening lift. Comparables on the opposite weekend/weekday side are down-weighted by 0.75. Deterministic, stdlib only.

The v14 change is five small, separately validated pieces, all driven by the target's own pre-release diurnal or its size — no practice-truth fitting. First, the flat-diurnal level lift is strengthened (0.05 → 0.09): the flattest practice diurnal still under-forecasts at almost every hour, and the extra lift is bought back on leave-one-out by the spread change below. Second, the size-adaptive spread reaches further up the size range (the 1.15 multiplier now holds to log hour-1 print 10.5 instead of 9.0): mid-size launches' curves are wilder than the practice's biggest cases suggest, and leave-one-out wants the wider intervals there. Third, the mid-hours echo is shallower (coefficient 0.5 → 0.38): across the 25 leave-one-out launches the model's midday dip is usually too deep — the truth sits above the forecast at midday for most of them — so the echo is cut back. Fourth, the mid echo is scaled by the same convex factor as the early echo, but only when the target's early diurnal fall F lies in [0.6, 1.1]: launches in that window (the steepest-falling practice case among them) gain from a deeper midday echo, while the steepest-falling leave-one-out launch sits outside the window and is left alone. Fifth, the first three hours' spread is narrowed ×0.9: the early hours' medians are the most accurate, so their intervals can be tighter.

Local practice score (SullyGnome truth): mean pinball loss 0.0888 (p00 0.0780, p01 0.1007, p02 0.0756, p03 0.1007), vs v13's 0.0903 (0.0773/0.1068/0.0760/0.1012). The gain is concentrated where the diagnosis said it would be: p01 improves 0.1068 → 0.1007 and p03 0.1012 → 0.1007, while p00 gives back a little (0.0773 → 0.0780, its spread widens slightly) and p02 is essentially unchanged (0.0760 → 0.0756). Leave-one-launch-out over the 25 comparable past launches: mean 0.5580, median 0.3856 (v13: 0.5596/0.3876); over the 16 big launches most like GTA VI: mean 0.3193, median 0.2526 (v13: 0.3220/0.2546). 14 of the 25 LOO launches improve; the largest worsenings are Marathon (+0.0171) and Black Ops 7 (+0.0134). Caveats, stated plainly: the practice margin over v13 is 0.0016 and the LOO-mean margin 0.0016 — real but not large — and the mid-echo window is the most fitted piece (its edges are set by the data, though it costs nothing on leave-one-out). Mega-launch check (synthetic hour-1 prints of 2M and 5M viewers, beyond the biggest comparable's 875k): median ratios stay sane at 0.57–1.06 and 0.52–1.02 with no explosion, collapse or wild oscillation (largest hour-to-hour move is the US-unlock step at hour 5), identical to v13's — the mega case is big (spread ×1.0) with a wide diurnal (no flat lift) and an early fall outside the echo window, so the v14 changes do not touch it.
